Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Luther is a private assistant app used only by its owner, Patrick Spanner. It is not available to the public.

What data it uses

With the owner's permission, Luther accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar and Apps Script) only to carry out tasks the owner asks for.

How data is handled

  • Data is used only to provide features the owner requested.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with third parties.
  • No person other than the owner reviews the data.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored securely and access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.

Luther's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the developer contact shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
